## Design decisions worth knowing

- **Stage 3 answer changed from the audit's proposal (STARS) to FIVE.**
  STARS appears in the puzzle's own text, so it would be copyable
  without doing the discrimination. FIVE requires combining both
  players' counts (3 + 2) — neither side alone can know it, and the
  word appears nowhere in either dossier. The "Five Stars" theme
  lives in the reveal instead.
- **Deliberately not used:** lawsuit, crypto scandals, mental-health
  disclosures, and the 2025 exodus material in the timeline. This is
  a fan game about the milestones.
- **Star mapping caveat (from the timeline doc itself):** the
  five-star mapping is "reconstructed from the crest files and the
  results record — an inference from the record rather than a
  statement from the organisation." The game's Stage 3 reveal says
  exactly that; it is not presented as an official FaZe claim.
- Every reveal modal cites the timeline as its source.

## What counts as verified

- A timeline entry supplied by the HackUTA / FaZe Clan sponsor, or a
  primary FaZe Clan source the sponsor points to.
- Anything else — including a plausible-sounding date or event — stays
  out until the sponsor confirms it.
